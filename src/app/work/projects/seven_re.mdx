## Overview

With rising urbanization, the demand for seamless, secure, and real-time public transportation systems has intensified. At SevenRE UG, we partnered with municipal agencies and the **German government** to deliver a smart mobility platform aimed at simplifying city travel — integrating digital ticketing, authentication, and communication services into one ecosystem.

As a Software Developer, I was instrumental in building cloud-native microservices, integrating third-party APIs, and ensuring platform security and scalability through modern DevOps and cloud practices.

## Objectives

- Build cloud-native microservices for public transport, ticketing, and access
- Ensure secure authentication through industry-standard identity providers
- Enable real-time messaging for ride updates and ticket confirmations
- Integrate with public infrastructure systems under government collaboration
- Deploy scalable backend using serverless and IaC principles

## Unique Opportunity: On-Site Collaboration in Germany

To deeply understand user behavior and optimize the product experience, I relocated to Germany for 2 months during the project. This enabled me to:

- Use the actual transport app on the ground and provide UX feedback
- Collaborate closely with German government teams and local stakeholders
- Align technical decisions with real-world commuter scenarios
- Build stronger communication and delivery sync between international teams

## Technologies Used

- **Languages**: Python, Node.js
- **Cloud & Infra**: AWS Lambda, DynamoDB, SQS, API Gateway, Terraform
- **Authentication**: Okta (OAuth 2.0, SSO)
- **Messaging**: Twilio (SMS & in-app notifications)
- **CI/CD & Deployment**: Terraform, GitHub Actions
- **Security**: Token-based access, GDPR-compliant design

## My Contributions

- Designed & built microservices on AWS Lambda with Terraform and DynamoDB
- Developed services to manage ticketing, access control, and usage analytics
- Integrated Okta for secure user authentication across multiple city-level apps
- Implemented Twilio messaging for ride updates, ticket confirmations, and emergency alerts
- Created infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) for reliable and consistent deployments
- Worked with product and city stakeholders to translate functional goals into technical solutions
- Ensured GDPR compliance in data handling and third-party integrations
- Built and tested fault-tolerant workflows, with retries and dead-letter handling via AWS SQS

## Collaboration & Government Partnership

- Contributed to a federally supported initiative aimed at smart city transportation
- Coordinated with government mobility teams and internal stakeholders to meet policy and tech compliance
- Delivered services used by thousands of users daily across municipal transportation networks

## Impact

- Reduced onboarding time for public transport partners by 40% through microservice-driven architecture
- Enabled real-time ticketing and alerting, improving commuter experience
- Scaled seamlessly to support multiple city partners and future transit integrations
- Delivered production-ready services within tight governmental timelines

## Learnings & Takeaways
